Quick guideHow to play

  1. 1
    Beat par

    Play 9 or 18 holes. Your score is your total strokes against par: E is level, −1 is one under, +1 is one over. Lower is better.

  2. 2
    Pick a shot

    Every shot you choose one option. Each shows a risk level and a roll range, like 7–16.

  3. 3
    Roll the dice

    The game rolls a number inside that range. Higher is better: 20 is perfect, 1 is a disaster. Your player's strengths and weaknesses add to or take from the roll.

  4. 4
    Safe or bold?

    Low risk has a narrow range, so no disasters but no fireworks. Higher risk has a wide range: a monster drive or the trees. Ultimate can be anything from 1 to 20, and can even hole out.

  5. 5
    Watch the course

    Water and out of bounds cost a penalty stroke. Rough, bunkers and trees make your next roll harder, so a bad shot can snowball.

  6. 6
    On the green

    Putts are measured in feet. Short putts need a low roll to drop, long ones need a big roll. Close misses become tap-ins.

  7. 7
    Score names

    One under is a birdie, two under an eagle, and a hole in one is the dream. Get to par plus 5 and your player picks up.
